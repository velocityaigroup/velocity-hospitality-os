# Velocity Hospitality OS — full system audit & remediation
**Date:** 20 August 2026 · **Scope:** the product exactly as it existed on disk, then the fixes applied.
**Rule followed:** audit before changing anything; no architectural rewrite; additive changes only.

---

## 1. What was audited

Every module, agent, workflow, integration, UI surface, HTTP endpoint and data state in
`04_Products/Velocity-Hospitality-OS/velocity-hospitality-os`, plus the Buildathon war-room and
Firefly design-partner packs that sit alongside it.

Verification method: read the source, then **run it** — `pytest`, `ruff`, `eval/run_eval.py`,
`demo/run_demo.py`, boot the console and exercise every endpoint including the failure paths, then
drive the browser UI headlessly and screenshot every view at three viewport sizes.

## 3. What was changed

Additive only. No agent contract, no loop phase, no retrieval parameter was altered.

### 3.1 Firefly Estate Bequia is now a configured property (D3)

`src/velocity_hos/knowledge/firefly.py` — **24 knowledge records across 9 departments**
(Property & Grounds, Accommodation, Villa Operations, Rates & Booking, Food & Beverage,
Activities & Golf, Guest FAQ, Arrival & Transport, Staff Operations), seeded from Firefly's own
public website, each carrying its source page.

**Governance, enforced in code, not just in prose:**

- Every record is tagged `confidence="unconfirmed"`, `approval_status="draft"` — and the console
  shows that badge on every answer. Two new optional schema fields (`confidence`, `source`) carry it.
- **11 declared knowledge gaps** (G1–G11: rates, restaurant hours, check-in times, transfer prices,
  day-pass policy, child/pet/accessibility policy, villa service path, booking notice periods, the
  unverified TripAdvisor figure, unharvested pages, and no documented maintenance procedure).
  These are deliberately **absent** from the corpus.
- A new **declared-gap guard** in the SOP Coach: a question landing in a declared gap is refused
  **by name** — *"I don't have a documented answer for that — no published room rates. Please check
  with the owner/manager."* — instead of being answered from a neighbouring record that happens to
  share vocabulary. It is opt-in per property, so the demonstration corpus is unaffected.
- A test asserts the product never describes Firefly as a live or paid pilot.

### 3.2 Property registry — real multi-tenancy (D3, D5)

`src/velocity_hos/knowledge/properties.py`. Each property carries its own corpus, gaps,
departments, team and one day of operational inputs. Corpora are **never merged**: a test asserts no
record id appears in both, and that Firefly answers only ever cite `FF-` sources. This is also what
protects the 46-SOP corpus's 95% metric — adding a second property costs it nothing.

### 3.3 Dates are generated relative to today (D4)

All operational inputs are produced from `date.today()`. A test fails if any hire's start date is in
the past. The demonstration cannot go stale.

### 3.4 One console, six views, four agents, real approvals (D1, D2, D5, D6, D7)

`ui/server.py` rebuilt. `ui/velocity_console.html` retired to `docs/legacy/` — the duplicate
client-side re-implementation is gone, so the two surfaces can no longer disagree.

- **Property switcher** in the header; every view is property-scoped.
- **Four agents** in the loop, including Work Order.
- **Approvals are real.** `POST /api/decide` resolves an item, writes the human decision *and* the
  execution to the audit trail, creates an **assigned task with an owner and an SLA**, and
  **re-runs the Executive Intelligence agent** over the new state — so the approved item moves out
  of "Awaiting your approval" and into "Actions taken" in the GM briefing, in front of the audience.
  That is the loop closing, and it is now demonstrable rather than described.
- **Reset demo** button (`POST /api/reset`, `POST /api/reset-all`).
- Views: Dashboard · SOP Coach · Operations Loop · Approvals · Knowledge Base · Knowledge Gaps · Evidence.
- Loading spinners, success confirmations, typed error states; every handler wrapped so a failure
  returns a message instead of killing the console mid-demonstration.
- Responsive at 390 px, 820 px and 1440 px — verified by screenshot.

### 3.5 Correctness and hygiene (D8–D12)

- Offline answer synthesis now skips the retrieval header and keyword line.
- Malformed JSON returns HTTP 400 with a message; bad indexes, bad decisions, double decisions and
  deciding before a cycle has run all return typed errors.
- Trail rows normalised to one schema; sequence numbers verified contiguous by test.
- Snapshots return copies, not live handles on server state.
- `ruff` clean.
- `demo/run_demo.py` now reads the property registry and takes `--property`.

The 33 new tests cover property isolation, provenance governance, the gap guard (including that it

reset.

**The Firefly 100% is on a 38-case seed corpus and is not comparable to the 62-case Azure Bay
number. Say that out loud when showing it.** The honest headline is the second and third rows: it
refuses every question the property has not documented, and names which gap it fell into.

## 5. What still needs Firefly

Nothing in the product is blocked, but eleven things are unanswerable until the operator supplies
them. They are listed in the console's **Knowledge Gaps** view, and each one is a question in the
discovery runbook: rates · restaurant hours and menu · check-in and check-out times · airport and
dock transfers · day-pass policy for non-residents · child, pet and accessibility policy · the villa
service-request path · booking notice periods for tables and tours · the TripAdvisor figure ·
remaining website pages · and how a maintenance defect is reported, owned and prioritised.

When those arrive: replace the seed records, flip `confidence` to `operator_confirmed`, re-run
`eval/firefly_eval.py`, and the console's "0 of 24 operator-confirmed" tile moves on its own.
